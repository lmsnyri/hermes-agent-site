Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Liam's Hermes agent ("the app") is a private assistant used only by its owner. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads and manages the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts, only to carry out tasks the owner asks for.

Where data is stored

Data is processed and stored on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in local files readable only by the owner's account.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone. It is not used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reaching the account; locally stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
